for choosing ABM.

01 / NUMBERS

Interest in Numbers & Financial Calculations

Multiple team members (Robmel, Acer, and Rene) explicitly chose ABM due to an enjoyment of math, solving problems, and calculating financial data.

02 / BUSINESS

Building Business & Entrepreneurial Foundations

Members (Gillian, Robmel, and Marvin) selected ABM to develop core business, strategy, and management skills needed to run or support enterprises.

03 / FINANCE

Financial Literacy & Wealth Management

Understanding how money works, managing wealth effectively, and achieving financial stability were driving factors (Rene, Acer).

04 / IMPACT

Personal & Altruistic Motivation

Giving back to parents and family (Rene) and creating a positive impact in communities through business (Robmel).
