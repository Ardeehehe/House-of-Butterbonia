03 / CAREER PATHS

Start small.
Grow with purpose.

This track can lead to many different jobs. Most people start with simple, entry-level roles to learn the basics. As they gain experience, they take on more responsibility and choose a specialty. Later, they can become senior experts or team leaders. The job titles may change, but the path is the same: start small, build skills, and grow into the role that fits you.

05 / RESOURCES & NEXT STEPS

KEEP
GOING.

To get started in this track, use online courses, tutorials, and free guides to learn the basic skills, and read books, blogs, or listen to podcasts from people already working in the field. Practice by doing small projects and create a simple portfolio to show what you’ve done, and you may join clubs, online groups, or local meetups to meet others and get advice.

Next, look for internships, part-time jobs, or volunteer work related to the track, and keep learning as the field changes-start small, stay curious, and keep going.
